2. Goals
Primary goals

The system must:

Accept bulk student data through CSV.
Validate and ingest the data.
Store student records securely.
Calculate derived academic metrics.
identify students requiring attention.
Generate an AI counselling brief.
Allow counsellors to record counselling sessions.
Generate structured action plans.
Track follow-up dates/status.
Compare student performance over time.
Identify measurable improvement or lack of improvement.
Provide a dashboard for counsellors.
Non-goals for V1

Do not build:

Automatic diagnosis of mental-health conditions.
Automatic parent communication.
Automatic disciplinary judgments.
Facial/emotion recognition.
Voice counselling.
WhatsApp automation.
ERP integration.
n8n workflows.
Multiple autonomous AI agents.
College-wide role/permission complexity beyond basic authentication.
