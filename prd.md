1. PRD — Product Requirements Document
1.1 Product Name

Student Counselling & Early Intervention Agent

Short description

A cloud-hosted web application that ingests bulk student academic data, identifies students who may require academic intervention, generates AI-assisted counselling briefs, records counselling sessions, creates action plans, tracks follow-ups, and measures improvement over time.

1.2 Problem

College counsellors may have to review large amounts of student information:

Attendance
Internal marks
SGPA/CGPA
Backlogs
Assignments/labs
Behaviour
Previous counselling

Manually identifying students requiring intervention and tracking their progress can be time-consuming.

The system provides a centralized workflow:

Student Data
     ↓
Analysis
     ↓
Intervention Detection
     ↓
Counselling
     ↓
Action Plan
     ↓
Follow-up
     ↓
Improvement Tracking
